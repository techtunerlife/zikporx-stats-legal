Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026 · Applies to the Zikporx Stats app for iPhone and iPad, including its widgets.

At a glance

  • We don't collect any data. TechTunerLife LLC runs no server for this app and receives nothing from it.
  • Your App Store Connect API key is stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • Your App Store data is downloaded directly from Apple and stored only on your device.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

1. Who we are

Zikporx Stats ("the app") is developed by TechTunerLife LLC ("we", "us"). Contact: support@techtunerlife.com.

2. Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software, and does not send any information to us or to any server operated by us. We cannot see your API key, your App Store data, or how you use the app.

3. Your App Store Connect API key

To show your statistics, you give the app an App Store Connect API key (the .p8 key file, Key ID and Issuer ID) and your vendor number. These are:

  • stored in the iOS Keychain on your device, marked for this device only (not synced through iCloud);
  • used only to sign requests that the app sends directly to Apple's App Store Connect API;
  • never sent to us or to any other party.

If you use the Paste key option, the app reads your clipboard only at the moment you tap that button, and then clears the clipboard so the key doesn't stay there.

4. Data the app downloads from Apple

Using your key, the app downloads information about your own apps from Apple, such as sales and download figures, proceeds, customer reviews (which may include a reviewer's nickname and country or region), ratings, App Store version status and App Analytics figures. This data:

  • is stored in a database on your device, in the app's own storage (shared only with the app's widgets on the same device);
  • is not uploaded anywhere and is not accessible to us.

5. Services the app connects to

The app connects directly to the following services. As with any internet connection, these services can see your device's IP address. Please see their own privacy policies.

  • Apple App Store Connect API (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com) and Apple's report downloads: to fetch your App Store data with your key. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple iTunes Lookup API (itunes.apple.com): to read the public star rating of your apps. Only the app IDs and a country code are sent.
  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev): to get European Central Bank exchange rates for converting proceeds to US dollars. Only currency codes and dates are sent.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net, serving the open-source currency-api): exchange rates for currencies the ECB doesn't publish. Only dates are sent.

6. Widgets

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets only read the data already stored on your device by the app. They don't connect to the internet or use your key.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your key and downloaded data stay on your device until you remove them. To delete everything, open Settings → Remove key and data from this iPhone in the app. Deleting the app also removes its stored data; because iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, we recommend using "Remove key and data" first. You can also revoke the key at any time in App Store Connect (Users and Access → Integrations).

8. Security

The key is protected by the iOS Keychain and all connections use encrypted HTTPS. Because the key gives access to your App Store Connect account, keep your device protected with a passcode and create keys only with the access you need.

9. Children

The app is a tool for app developers and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children; in fact, we collect no information at all.

10. Your rights

Because we don't collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. All data is under your control on your device, as described above. If you have questions, contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
